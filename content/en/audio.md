# Listen to the suttas

Hear the suttas read aloud, in English, and follow the text as the voice reads it.

## What you hear

The text is Bhikkhu Sujato's English translation, published by SuttaCentral. The voice is synthetic, and the text follows the voice as it reads.

## How it grows

About 1,000 suttas are narrated today, and more are added every week.

## Where the text comes from

The translation comes from SuttaCentral, which publishes it under the Creative Commons CC0 dedication. The voice is made with Chatterbox, by Resemble AI, under the MIT licence.
