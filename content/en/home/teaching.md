The Tipiṭaka, the "three baskets", is the oldest record of the Buddha's teaching, more than twenty-five centuries old: the Four Noble Truths and the path to the end of suffering.

Read it in Pāli and in translation, with a dictionary on every word, in the Tipiṭaka app.
