# About PaliCanon

PaliCanon is a free and non-commercial project. It aims to make the Pāli Canon easy to read, search and study, for everyone and in several languages.

## The texts

The English translations are by Bhikkhu Sujato and the Pāli text is the Mahāsaṅgīti edition, both published by [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net). Word meanings come from the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary by Bhikkhu Bodhirasa.

## With gratitude

To the translators, to the SuttaCentral team and to everyone who keeps these texts alive.
