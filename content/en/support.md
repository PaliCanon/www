# Support PaliCanon

PaliCanon is free, without ads and without tracking. If it helps you, you can help it too.

- **Make a donation** on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/tipitaka), one-off or monthly: it goes straight toward the costs below.
- **Rate the app** on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760995639).
- **Follow the daily sutta** on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/daily_sutta).
- **Share it** with someone who might like it.

## What it costs

- The server that runs the sites, the search and its meaning-based index.
- The domain name and the network in front of the site.
- The Apple developer membership that keeps the app on the App Store.
- The computing to build and rebuild the indexes and the translations.
- Time: building, fixing, adding languages.

## Other ways to help

The Dhamma has always been offered freely; this keeps the offering going.
