# Support PaliCanon

PaliCanon is free, without ads and without tracking. If it helps you, you can help it too.

- **Make a donation** on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/tipitaka).
- **Rate the app** on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760995639).
- **Follow the daily sutta** on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/daily_sutta).
- **Share it** with someone who might like it.
