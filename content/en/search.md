# Search the Pāli Canon

Type a few words you remember, or a question in your own words. The search finds the passages that match, with the sutta they come from and a link to read it on SuttaCentral.

- **By sutta**: a reference such as MN 10 or a title opens the text straight away.
- **By keywords**: the exact words you remember, in Pāli or in translation.
- **By meaning**: a question in your own words, such as "how to deal with anger", and the passages that answer it.

## How it works

Two engines read the Canon, and the search combines them.

The first works on **words**: a lexical index of every passage, in Pāli and in translation, which finds the exact terms you typed and weighs how rare they are. It is precise, and it needs the right word.

The second works on **meaning**: every passage has been placed in an embedding space, a space of thousands of dimensions where texts that say similar things sit close together. Your question is placed in the same space, and the nearest passages come back, even when they use none of your words.

The two lists are merged by rank, so a passage found by both comes first. You can also choose one engine alone: words, or meaning.
