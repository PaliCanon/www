# Learn Pāli

Pāli is the language in which the Buddha's teaching was kept, recited and written down. To read a sutta in Pāli is to meet the words themselves, before any translation: dukkha, sati, mettā, nibbāna. Learn Pāli takes you there one word at a time.

## How it works

The deck holds the words the suttas use most, with their meaning from the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary, their forms and a line from a sutta where they appear. Each word is asked four ways:

- **Meaning**: the Pāli word, and you give its sense.
- **Recall**: the sense, and you give the Pāli word.
- **Forms**: a declension or a conjugation of the word.
- **Context**: a sutta line with the word blanked out, to find it in its place.

A word you know moves to a longer rhythm, from daily to weekly, monthly and beyond; a word you forget comes back the next day. A few minutes a day are enough.

## Where you start

The first words are the ones you will meet on every page of the Canon: the particles and pronouns, the verbs of saying and knowing, the names of the teaching. From there the deck follows the suttas, so what you learn is what you read.

## What stays on your side

Your progress stays in your browser: no account, nothing sent anywhere. Clear it and you start over.
