# The Pāli Canon, open to everyone

PaliCanon gathers free tools to read, search and learn the Pāli Canon, the oldest collection of the Buddha's teachings.

## Search the Canon

Find a passage by the words you remember or by what you mean. [Open the search](https://search.palicanon.app)

## Learn Pāli

Learn the language of the suttas, one word at a time. [Discover Learn Pāli](https://learn.palicanon.app)

## Tipiṭaka on iPhone

The whole Canon in your pocket, even offline. [Get the app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760995639)
