## Privacy

### Learn: syncing your progress with Google Drive

Learn users can optionally sync their progress with their own Google Drive. This sync only happens when you sign in with Google. The scope is limited to a hidden folder (`drive.appdata`) that only PaliCanon can access. Your progress is stored in this folder in the same format as the Export feature.

Your Google sign-in token is kept in your browser's memory (on the site) or in the macOS Keychain (on the app), never on PaliCanon's servers. PaliCanon has no server-side copy of your token or your data. Signing out removes the access when the token is still stored. On the app, it's always stored. On the site, it's stored only until you reload the page. You can remove the access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.

Google's privacy policy applies to how Google processes your data. According to Google's Limited Use requirements, the data from Google APIs is used only to provide this sync feature.
