## Header spec (v3)
- H1 desktop (from 80rem, measured in fr and vi with no wrap): one bar 84rem max, 4rem tall, inset; brand (decorative lotus + "PaliCanon" serif, one link "PaliCanon, home"); the 7 page links right-aligned, Inter 15/500, the current page on a chip (`aria-current=page`); a 1px divider; the language control.
- H2 language control: a `nav` labelled Language / Langue / Ngôn ngữ holding a segmented pill (1px outline, radius 999, 3px padding): English / Français / Tiếng Việt in their own names, Inter 14/600, each with `lang`, segments ≥ 2.25rem; the current language a filled gold segment (`span`, `aria-current=true`), the others links with `hreflang`, chip on hover, writing `pc_lang`; no flags, codes or grey words; a missing translation left out.
- H3 below 80rem: a 3.5rem bar, brand + a gold "Menu" pill (`popovertarget`) reading "Close" with × while open (`:has(#menu:popover-open)`); the panel a glass popover: the language pill first at full width (2.75rem segments), then the 7 links in 44px rows; non-modal, Escape and outside tap close it, focus returns to the pill.
- H4 states: at the top the bar sits on the deep card (no glass, light text, gold segment); past 4rem of scroll a glass pill (opacity 0.92, blur 18px, shadow) by scroll-driven CSS, `data-scrolled` set by story.ts where unsupported and at load; hides on scroll down past the first screen, returns on scroll up and at the top; CSS keeps it shown on `:focus-within` and while the menu is open (glass forced); never hides under the pointer or under reduced motion.
- H5 contrast (worst cases): light glass over the deep card: heading 13.5:1, current fill #7a5f14 4.8:1 on the bar, parchment text on it 5.6:1; dark glass over a white screenshot: text 8.2:1, gold fill 4.4:1, ink on it 6.7:1; on the deep card: text 12.6:1, gold 6.7:1; focus ring 2px gold, offset 2px.
- H6 no JS: the bar fixed and always shown, the popover menu works; without popover support the Menu pill drops and the links wrap in a static bar; the footer repeats the page links and the languages.
- Also from the rounds: S1 a scene's heading stays pinned with its steps, no blank screen between scenes; O2 the Vietnamese line height checked against the band and the comma; slider images lazy-loaded; AA for the phone glass cards over the drawing; languages in the footer.
- Simplify (orchestrator ruling, no voice answered): keep the opening card's scaling on scroll (the user asked for immersive); drop the 0.8 KB fallback module (browsers without scroll-driven animations get the static layout; one code path less).
